# Publication methods audit

Reviewed 12 September 2026. This working audit maps the research designs and statistical approaches reported in Olanrewaju Eniade's article record. It supports the portfolio's aggregated methods section and does not imply sole responsibility for every method in a co-authored study.

The ORCID export contained 20 research articles or preprints suitable for methods extraction. A conference supplement abstract and an "ongoing and completed projects" record were excluded. The 2026 HIV funding paper, which was not yet in the retrieved ORCID export, was also reviewed.

| Year | Publication | Reported design and methods |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2026 | Projected Impact of International HIV Funding Withdrawal... | Deterministic compartmental HIV transmission model; ordinary differential equations; calibration to Nigeria-specific indicators; five funding scenarios; Monte Carlo probabilistic sensitivity analysis; one-way sensitivity and tornado analysis; two-dimensional interaction heatmaps; 95% uncertainty intervals; reproducible R pipeline. |
| 2026 | Development and Prototype Implementation of a Dehydration Risk Prediction Model... | Evidence-based model development; triangulation of guidelines, validated scales and meta-analytic estimates; heuristic weighting; logistic probability mapping; risk stratification; simulation-based prototype testing; R Shiny implementation. This is a preprint and the model is explicitly provisional. |
| 2025 | Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis of Risk Factors for Dehydration... | PROSPERO-registered systematic review following the JBI manual; PRISMA selection; Newcastle-Ottawa and Cochrane risk-of-bias tools; pooled diagnostic-performance estimates; sensitivity analysis; predictive scoring framework. |
| 2025 | Bullying and its Effects on Mental Health... | Cross-sectional prevalence study; simple random sampling; validated APRI and SDQ instruments; descriptive statistics; Fisher's exact test. |
| 2024 | Psychometric Properties of the Knowledge of Hydration... | Online cross-sectional instrument-development study; Cronbach's alpha reliability; factor analysis and rotated component matrix; intraclass correlation; chi-square testing. |
| 2024 | Factors Associated with HPV Genital Warts... | Self-reported online cross-sectional study; descriptive statistics; chi-square and Fisher's exact tests; binary logistic regression with adjusted odds ratios and 95% confidence intervals. |
| 2023 | Prevalence and associated factors of hypertension among type 2 diabetes mellitus patients... | Hospital-based retrospective study using secondary records with additional structured lifestyle data; simple random sampling; descriptive statistics; chi-square tests; binary logistic regression. |
| 2023 | Time-to-Survival from COVID-19 Disease... | Retrospective analysis of patient records; Kaplan-Meier estimation; log-rank tests; Cox regression; hazard ratios and 95% confidence intervals. |
| 2022 | Perceptions of COVID-19 transmission risk and testing readiness... | Community-based cross-sectional survey; multistage cluster sampling; household wealth index by principal component analysis; binary logistic regression; sandwich estimator for cluster-robust standard errors. |
| 2022 | Inaccessibility to Healthcare Services amidst COVID-19 Pandemic... | Secondary analysis of PMA cross-sectional survey data from three countries; multistage cluster design; descriptive statistics; chi-square tests; modelling of healthcare inaccessibility. |
| 2022 | Compliance with COVID-19 Prevention and Risky Sexual Behaviour... | Cross-sectional youth survey using KoboToolbox; descriptive statistics; chi-square tests; binary logistic regression with adjusted odds ratios. |
| 2022 | COVID-19 Vaccine Uptake, Unmet Need and Reported Side Effect... | National online cross-sectional survey using KoboToolbox; descriptive analysis; binary logistic regression. |
| 2022 | Covid-19 Psychological Trauma and its Associated Factors among Nurses... | Online cross-sectional study; Kessler-10 psychological distress scale; descriptive statistics; chi-square tests; binary logistic regression. |
| 2021 | Risk factors of anemia among selected countries... | Secondary analysis of DHS data; binary logistic regression; random-effects generalised linear modelling; three-way interaction analysis. |
| 2021 | Influencing factors of unmet needs for child spacing... | Secondary analysis of DHS Phase VII data from three countries; descriptive statistics; chi-square tests; binary logistic regression. |
| 2021 | Partner's profile and unmet need for child limiting... | Cross-sectional analysis using a pre-tested structured questionnaire; chi-square or independent t-tests; staged multiple logistic regression; adjusted odds ratios and 95% confidence intervals. |
| 2021 | Determinants of Immunization Completeness... | Secondary analysis of Nigeria DHS records; descriptive statistics; generalised linear modelling for a binary immunisation outcome. |
| 2021 | Effect of Education on Attitude Towards Domestic Violence... | Secondary analysis of MICS data; multinomial logistic regression for selection bias; propensity score weighting; covariate-balance diagnostics; regression adjustment with inverse-probability weighting; average treatment effects. |
| 2021 | Knowledge, Attitude and Prevention Practice of COVID-19... | Online cross-sectional survey using KoboToolbox; scale reliability using Cronbach's alpha; descriptive statistics; tests of independence; binary logistic regression. |
| 2021 | Willingness to Accept COVID-19 Vaccine and Its Determinants... | Web-based cross-sectional study; descriptive statistics; chi-square tests; binary logistic regression. |
| 2021 | Infertility and Its Associated Factors... | Matched case-control study across three health facilities; descriptive and bivariate association analysis; odds ratios and 95% confidence intervals for associated factors. |
