01 / THE PERSON BEHIND THE ANALYSIS

A research mind.
A practical approach.

10+

years connecting data,
research, and public health

Good analysis starts with understanding the question—and the people behind it.

My work sits at the intersection of biostatistics, epidemiology, and health data science. I work across clinical, laboratory, surveillance, and population-health datasets, connecting study design, data quality, statistical analysis, and reporting.

As Senior Data Scientist at the International Foundation Against Infectious Disease in Nigeria (IFAIN), I lead data-management, analysis, and quality activities for multi-site research. My experience spans antimicrobial resistance, vaccine safety, HIV, maternal and child health, and non-communicable diseases.

I have supported programmes involving international funders and research partners, including NIH, Gavi, Merck, Wellcome, and the Wellcome Sanger Institute. Whether your need is a focused analysis or a longer collaboration, I bring reproducible methods, clear communication, and attention to what the results mean.
